Questions

Frequently asked questions.

How do I make an Indian passport size photo at home?

Open llemonAI Photo Editor, go to the Passport tab and choose a photo. India passport 35×45 mm is the default: the face is found, the background is turned white and the head is sized to the official guide lines automatically. Print a sheet of 8 photos on 6×4 inch paper, or save a 630×810 file under 250 KB for the Passport Seva online form.

What is the size of an Indian passport photo?

An Indian passport photo is 35×45 mm (3.5×4.5 cm) on a white background. For the Passport Seva online application the app saves 630×810 pixels under 250 KB. India visa and OCI photos are 2×2 inch (51×51 mm). Always check the latest official rules before applying.

How do I remove the background from a photo for free?

Open the photo, go to Edit → Background and tap Remove background. On-device AI finds the person and removes the background without uploading anything. Then keep it transparent (PNG), choose white or any colour, blur it, or place the person on any scenery photo.

How do I change a photo background to white?

In Edit → Background tap Remove background and choose white. For passport photos the Passport tab does this automatically. A Refine brush touches up hair and edges.

How do I reduce a photo size in KB, for example to 20 KB, 50 KB, 100 KB or 250 KB?

Click Save, choose JPG and type a Maximum file size in KB. The app finds the best quality that fits the limit, and lowers the resolution only if needed. Example: a 4000×1824 camera photo of 1.4 MB was saved at 246 KB with a 250 KB limit.

How do I change the resolution or DPI of a photo?

Go to Edit → Crop → Image size & resolution. Choose a quick size (4K, Full HD, HD, WhatsApp / email, Small, 50%, 25%) or type the size in pixels, percent, cm, mm or inches, and set the DPI (for example 300 for printing). A before / after table shows pixels, print size, DPI and file size before you apply. With Resample off only the print size changes.

How do I resize a photo in cm for a form or for printing?

In Edit → Crop → Image size & resolution choose cm (or mm or inches), type the width or height and the DPI, and apply. Saved JPG and PNG files keep their DPI, so they print at exactly that size.

How do I make a photo collage on A4 or 4×6 paper?

Open the Collage tab, choose a page from 6×4 inch to A4 or Letter (portrait or landscape) and one of 24 layouts, then add photos. Drag to swap, pan and zoom inside each box, set spacing, margins and rounded corners, and print at exact size or save JPG, PNG or PDF at 300 DPI.

Does llemonAI Photo Editor work offline, and are my photos uploaded?

It works fully offline. Photos are opened, edited and saved on your device, and the AI models are built into the app, so nothing is uploaded. It only goes online when you click Check for updates.

Is llemonAI Photo Editor free? Which devices does it run on?

Yes, it is free with no watermark and no sign-up. It runs on Windows 10 and 11 (64-bit and 32-bit, one portable .exe), macOS 12 and later (Apple Silicon and Intel) and Android 8.0 and later.

Opening llemonAI Image Editor on a Mac

The Mac app isn't notarized by Apple (that needs a paid Apple developer account), so macOS blocks it the first time you open it. You only have to allow it once:

  1. Open the downloaded .dmg and drag llemonAI Image Editor into the Applications folder. Open it from Applications, not from the disk image.
  2. Double-click the app. macOS says “Apple could not verify ‘llemonAI Image Editor’ is free of malware that may harm your Mac or compromise your privacy.” Click Done.
  3. Open System Settings → Privacy & Security and scroll down to Security. Next to “‘llemonAI Image Editor’ was blocked to protect your Mac”, click Open Anyway.
  4. Enter your Mac password (or use Touch ID), then click Open Anyway once more.

After that it opens normally with a double-click, and updates installed from About → Check for updates open without asking again. The warning doesn't mean anything was found in the app; you can confirm your download is genuine with its SHA256 checksum.

macOS 14 Sonoma or earlier, or using Terminal

On macOS 12–14 you can also right-click (or Control-click) the app in Applications, choose Open, then Open again.

Or, in Terminal, remove the “downloaded from the internet” mark (one time): xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine "/Applications/llemonAI Image Editor.app"

To check a download, run certutil -hashfile llemonAI-Image-Editor-1.0.0-win-x64.exe SHA256 on Windows or shasum -a 256 llemonAI-Image-Editor-1.0.0-macos.dmg on a Mac, and compare it with the checksum above. The files are signed with llemonai's own certificates, not a public authority, so Windows SmartScreen may say "Unknown Publisher" and Android may ask you to allow installs from this source. Both are expected.
