Why two shelves

Fintech and tech don't read the same way

One product lives or dies on technical depth — protocol internals, signal timing, chipset quirks. The other lives or dies on trust — clear numbers, calm paperwork, a claim sheet your family can actually use. Forcing both onto one palette would undersell them both, so the Scanner stays dark and technical below, and the Vault stays light and paper-like, the way it already looks inside the product. The llemonai wordmark is the only thing they share.
